# Frontend Homepage Content Prompts

## Homepage Content Strategy for Beta Coefficient Forecasting Platform

### 🎯 Core Value Proposition
**Primary Message**: "Predict stock beta coefficients with advanced ARIMA forecasting to understand future market sensitivity"

**Target Audience**: 
- Quantitative analysts
- Portfolio managers
- Investment professionals
- Financial researchers

### 📝 Content Guidelines

#### Hero Section
- **Focus**: Beta coefficient prediction capabilities
- **Keywords**: ARIMA forecasting, market sensitivity, statistical modeling
- **CTA**: Direct users to Beta Dashboard as primary action

#### Value Propositions (Why Choose This Platform)
1. **Predictive Market Sensitivity**: Anticipate stock-market correlations
2. **Advanced Time Series Modeling**: ARIMA + OLS regression methodology  
3. **Statistical Validation**: R², p-values, confidence intervals

#### Features Section (What We Offer)
1. **ARIMA Forecasting**: Autoregressive integrated moving average models
2. **OLS Regression**: Statistical validation and significance testing
3. **Time Series Analysis**: Rolling windows and confidence bands
4. **Market Sensitivity**: Correlation predictions over time

#### How It Works (Process Flow)
1. **Upload Historical Price Data**: CSV with dates, tickers, prices
2. **ARIMA Model Forecasting**: Advanced statistical modeling
3. **Interactive Beta Dashboard**: Real-time forecasts and visualizations
